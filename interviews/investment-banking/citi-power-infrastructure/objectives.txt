# Objectives for Citi · Power, Utilities & Infrastructure (4 steps).
# "branches" maps a next objective to the condition that activates it (Tavus next_conditional_objectives).

name: conduct_ib_power_infra_interview
prompt: |
  Run a realistic first-round investment banking interview for a power, utilities and infrastructure coverage team: name and target role, motivation (why IB, why Citi, why the sector), one or two CV deep-dives, three to five core technicals, two sector questions, the utility financing case from the knowledge base, a market-awareness question, one behavioral question, and the candidate's questions. No feedback or praise during this step.
confirmation_mode: auto
branches: |
  end_early: Only when the candidate explicitly asks to stop, end, or leave.
  deliver_debrief: Only when the interview portion including the candidate's questions is complete, or the candidate asks for the debrief.
---
name: deliver_debrief
prompt: |
  Step out of role. Give verdict, six scores with reasons (motivation, technicals, sector knowledge, financing judgment, precision, communication), two strengths with quotes, three improvements with one model answer, corrections on missed technical or case calculations, the top topics to review, and delivery notes from perception.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: offer_redo_and_close
---
name: offer_redo_and_close
prompt: |
  Offer to redo the weakest answer once, give two sentences of feedback on the redo, then close warmly and wait for a goodbye before ending the call.
confirmation_mode: auto
---
name: end_early
prompt: |
  Use end_call immediately with reason "user_requested".
confirmation_mode: auto
