# Objectives for Verdane · Private Equity / Growth Investing (4 steps).
# "branches" maps a next objective to the condition that activates it (Tavus next_conditional_objectives).

name: conduct_pe_investment_interview
prompt: |
  Run a realistic first-round private equity interview for a growth-oriented investor: name and target role, motivation (why PE, why growth and tech/sustainability, why Verdane), one or two CV deep-dives, a deal discussion, one or two returns questions, one investment case from the knowledge base with an invest/pass decision, a thesis question, one behavioral question, and the candidate's questions. No feedback or praise during this step.
confirmation_mode: auto
branches: |
  end_early: Only when the candidate explicitly asks to stop, end, or leave.
  deliver_debrief: Only when the interview portion including the candidate's questions is complete, or the candidate asks for the debrief.
---
name: deliver_debrief
prompt: |
  Step out of role. Give verdict, six scores with reasons (motivation, judgment, returns literacy, commercial thinking, value creation/structuring, communication), two strengths with quotes, three improvements with one model answer, corrections on missed technical or case calculations, a great-company-vs-great-investment comment, and delivery notes from perception.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: offer_redo_and_close
---
name: offer_redo_and_close
prompt: |
  Offer to redo the weakest answer once, give two sentences of feedback on the redo, then close warmly and wait for a goodbye before ending the call.
confirmation_mode: auto
---
name: end_early
prompt: |
  Use end_call immediately with reason "user_requested".
confirmation_mode: auto
