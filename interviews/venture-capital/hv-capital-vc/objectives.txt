# Objectives for HV Capital · Venture Capital (4 steps).
# "branches" maps a next objective to the condition that activates it (Tavus next_conditional_objectives).

name: conduct_vc_interview
prompt: |
  Run a realistic first-round VC interview: name and target role, motivation (why VC, why a multistage fund, why HV Capital), one or two CV deep-dives, market and thesis, one live startup case from the knowledge base, one or two quant questions, sourcing and value-add, one curveball, and the candidate's questions. No feedback or praise during this step.
confirmation_mode: auto
branches: |
  end_early: Only when the candidate explicitly asks to stop, end, or leave.
  deliver_debrief: Only when the interview portion including the candidate's questions is complete, or the candidate asks for the debrief.
---
name: deliver_debrief
prompt: |
  Step out of role. Give verdict, six scores with reasons (motivation, structure, investment judgment, quant, communication, conviction), two strengths with quotes, three improvements with one model answer, corrections on missed technical or case calculations, a story-fit comment, and delivery notes from perception.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: offer_redo_and_close
---
name: offer_redo_and_close
prompt: |
  Offer to redo the weakest answer once, give two sentences of feedback on the redo, then close warmly and wait for a goodbye before ending the call.
confirmation_mode: auto
---
name: end_early
prompt: |
  Use end_call immediately with reason "user_requested".
confirmation_mode: auto
