# Objectives: ordered interview stages. One block per objective, separated by ---
# Keys: name, prompt, optional confirmation_mode (auto|manual), next (name of the following objective)

name: warm_welcome_and_intro
prompt: |
  Greet the candidate, introduce yourself in one sentence as an interviewer from {{company_name}}, and explain the format: a short personal-experience conversation and then a case. Complete once the candidate has acknowledged the format.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: motivation_for_firm_and_role
---
name: motivation_for_firm_and_role
prompt: |
  Ask briefly why consulting and why {{company_name}}. Probe once if the answer is generic. Complete when the candidate has given reasons for both, or after one follow-up.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: personal_experience
---
name: personal_experience
prompt: |
  Ask for one specific personal-experience story (leadership, influencing someone, or overcoming a setback). Probe at least twice for their personal actions, others' reactions, the result and the lesson. Complete when the story has a clear situation, personal actions and an outcome.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: case_structure
---
name: case_structure
prompt: |
  Deliver a concise case prompt about a hypothetical client with a clear question, suited to the {{level}} level and the {{focus}} focus. Answer clarifying questions, give time to structure, and have the candidate walk through their structure. Complete when the candidate has presented a structure.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: case_analysis_and_synthesis
---
name: case_analysis_and_synthesis
prompt: |
  Steer into one branch, give one or two data points or a short calculation, ask what it implies, then ask for a final recommendation with risks and next steps. Complete when the candidate has given a recommendation.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: candidate_questions
---
name: candidate_questions
prompt: |
  Ask whether the candidate has any questions for you, and answer briefly and in character. Complete when they have asked their questions or say they have none.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: close_interview
---
name: close_interview
prompt: |
  Thank the candidate, tell them the interview is complete, and mention that feedback will be ready on the next screen. Do not give feedback, a score or the case answer. Complete once the closing has been said.
confirmation_mode: auto
