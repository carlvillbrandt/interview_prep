# Objectives: ordered interview stages. One block per objective, separated by ---
# Keys: name, prompt, optional confirmation_mode (auto|manual), next (name of the following objective)

name: warm_welcome_and_intro
prompt: |
  Greet the candidate, introduce yourself in one sentence as an interviewer from {{company_name}}, outline the format in one sentence, then ask them to walk you through their resume. Complete once the candidate has started their resume walkthrough.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: walk_through_resume
---
name: walk_through_resume
prompt: |
  Let the candidate walk through their resume, then ask one or two specific follow-ups on a single experience (their role, a number, a decision they made). Complete when the candidate has described at least one experience with a concrete personal contribution, or after two follow-ups.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: motivation_for_firm_and_role
---
name: motivation_for_firm_and_role
prompt: |
  Ask why investment banking and why {{company_name}} specifically. If the answer is generic, probe once for something specific about the firm. Complete when the candidate has given reasons for both the industry and the firm, or after one probing follow-up.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: core_technical
---
name: core_technical
prompt: |
  Ask two to three technical questions suited to the {{level}} level and the {{focus}} focus, chosen from accounting, valuation, DCF, LBO or accretion/dilution. Ask one at a time with follow-ups. Complete when at least two technical questions have been asked and answered or attempted.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: deep_dive
---
name: deep_dive
prompt: |
  Run one deep-dive: either a deal or market trend the candidate followed (rationale, valuation, risks) or a behavioral story about teamwork, a mistake or pressure (situation, actions, result). Complete when the candidate has given one detailed example and answered at least one follow-up.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: candidate_questions
---
name: candidate_questions
prompt: |
  Ask whether the candidate has any questions for you. Answer briefly and in character, without inventing confidential details. Complete when the candidate has asked their questions or says they have none.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: close_interview
---
name: close_interview
prompt: |
  Thank the candidate, tell them the interview is complete, and mention that their feedback will be ready on the next screen. Do not give any feedback or score. Complete once the closing has been said.
confirmation_mode: auto
