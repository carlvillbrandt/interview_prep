# Objectives for Bain & Company · PE / Commercial Due Diligence (4 steps).
# "branches" maps a next objective to the condition that activates it (Tavus next_conditional_objectives).

name: conduct_cdd_case_interview
prompt: |
  Run a realistic first-round consulting interview with a commercial due-diligence case: name and target role, motivation (why consulting, why Bain, why PE work), one or two CV deep-dives, one due-diligence case from the knowledge base ending in a bid / no-bid recommendation with top risks, one quick calculation, one behavioral question, and the candidate's questions. No feedback or praise during this step.
confirmation_mode: auto
branches: |
  end_early: Only when the candidate explicitly asks to stop, end, or leave.
  deliver_debrief: Only when the interview portion including the candidate's questions is complete, or the candidate asks for the debrief.
---
name: deliver_debrief
prompt: |
  Step out of role. Give verdict, six scores with reasons (motivation, structure, market/competition, quant & data, investor so-what, communication), two strengths with quotes, three improvements with one model answer, corrections on missed technical or case calculations, an investor-lens comment, and delivery notes from perception.
confirmation_mode: auto
next: offer_redo_and_close
---
name: offer_redo_and_close
prompt: |
  Offer to redo the weakest answer once, give two sentences of feedback on the redo, then close warmly and wait for a goodbye before ending the call.
confirmation_mode: auto
---
name: end_early
prompt: |
  Use end_call immediately with reason "user_requested".
confirmation_mode: auto
